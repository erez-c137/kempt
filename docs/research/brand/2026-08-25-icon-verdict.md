# Icon verdict - candidates panel, 2026-08-25

Three independent judges (Breeze/KDE icon designer, brand designer, skeptical Fedora KDE user)
plus the controller reviewed six candidates in `candidates/`: two comb variants (Gemini cream
comb on slate; ChatGPT white comb on bright blue) and a 2x2 grid of generic update concepts
(Tux in refresh ring, shield + arrow, monitor + package, gear + arrow).

## Consensus

- **Direction: the comb.** Unanimous. It is the only concept that says "kempt" rather than
  "update", and the only one with no Breeze collision at full size.
- **Base: the Gemini variant** (3 of 4). Better spine-to-teeth proportion (36/64 vs 44/56, which
  drifts toward Breeze's `measure` ruler), and an ownable two-colour palette: slate #1F5885 with
  cream #F2F0E3 measures 6.3:1 contrast, higher than white on bright blue (5.7:1), and is absent
  from every store grid, where the bright blue reads as a KDE system component.
- **The four grid concepts are discarded.** Every one duplicates an existing Breeze icon
  (`view-refresh`, `security-high`, `computer`, `configure`), and Tux is a third-party mascot.
- **None of the six is shippable as drawn.** All are opaque rasters, and both combs collapse into
  a single bar at 16 and 22 px (about 17 hairline teeth = 1.0 px pitch on a 16 px canvas). A
  lone bar reads as a MINUS sign, the worst possible misread for an updater. A 5-tooth redraw
  resolves crisply at the same sizes: a drawing failure, not a concept failure.

## Redraw specification (binding for the SVGs)

Full-colour app icon (store, GitHub avatar, README):
- Slate tile #1F5885, cream comb #F2F0E3, two flat colours only, no gradient, bevel or shadow.
- True squircle with transparent corners (not a soft octagon); survives a circle crop.
- Comb fills about 72% of the tile width and 55% of its height (today: 78% x 26%, so it reads
  as a detail next to the wordmark).
- 5 teeth (4 as fallback if 125%/150% scaling merges them); tooth width equal to gap width;
  spine-to-teeth ratio kept at 36/64; square tooth tips (max 0.5 px radius at small sizes).

Panel glyph (`kempt-symbolic.svg`, monochrome, Breeze style):
- Pure `currentColor` fill, no tile, so it inherits every colour scheme.
- Two hand-hinted drawings, 16 px and 22 px, never one scaled: 16 px target about 14 w x 10 h
  (3 px spine, 6-7 px teeth, 5 teeth at 2 px stroke / 1 px gap = 14 px exactly).
- Keep clear of Breeze `actions/22/measure` (ruler): proportion + square tips do this.
- Reserve the bottom-right quadrant for the count badge: shorten the last tooth or shift the
  glyph 1 px up-left in the 22 px drawing.
- The optional down-chevron accent is for 48 px and above only.

Renders that back this up live with the judges' scratch output for the session and are
regenerated by the legibility sheet in `candidates/legibility/`.

## Ends decision (2026-08-25, later)

**Flush ends, and the comb grown vertically to pay for them.** The redraw above shipped with a
20 px spine overhang at each side, so the comb ended on a bar rather than on a tooth. It now ends
on a tooth: the spine stops exactly where the outermost teeth stop (x 56-200), and the height the
overhang was contributing is paid back downward - the comb grew from 140 px to 160 px tall
(y 58-198 becomes y 48-208), split 56 px spine / 104 px teeth. Every coordinate stays a multiple
of 8. Final: 144 x 160 = 56.3% x 62.5% of the tile, spine:teeth 35:65, square tips.

The constraint is that only **two of three** can hold at once - a 16 px tooth pitch, flush ends,
and the old 184 px footprint. Tooth pitch is the one that cannot give: at 32 px, 16 px pitch means
each tooth is exactly 2 px of solid cream and each gap exactly 2 px of solid slate. A row through
the teeth measures `240 240 | 88 88 | 240 240 | 88 88 | ...` with not one antialiased half-tone in
it, and the spine ends land hard on the same columns as the outer teeth. Stretching the pitch to
20/21 to keep the footprint (drawn as the discarded `kempt-flush-wide.svg`) put the tooth edges on
half-pixels and greyed them out at exactly the size the panel uses. So the footprint gave, and the
lost optical weight came back as height rather than as a wider, blurrier comb.

Flush also fixes what the overhang was costing at a glance: a spine running past its teeth reads
as a lintel over five columns, not as a comb. Ends that stop on a tooth read as a comb.

The distinction from Breeze `actions/22/measure` (the ruler) is unaffected. The squint test at
16 and 22 px separates the two on proportion and on the teeth being solid, long and open at the
bottom - a ruler is a hollow outlined box with short ticks hanging inside it. Nothing in that
comparison ever rested on the ends being ragged, so removing the overhang costs no distinctness.
The 22 px glyph follows the same call (spine x 2-16 y 3-8, teeth y 8-16 at 2 px / 1 px, glyph now
14 w); `kempt-symbolic-16.svg` was already flush and is unchanged.

Evidence: `candidates/legibility/kempt-svg/comparison-ends.png` (overhang vs flush, side by side)
and `candidates/legibility/kempt-svg/final-sheet.png` (the shipped drawing at 128/64/48/32 on
Breeze light and dark, plus nearest-neighbour zooms).
